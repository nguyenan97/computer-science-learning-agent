# IUH Master of Computer Science — Condensed Curriculum

> **Source basis:** IUH Faculty of Information Technology, *Outline of Master Program in Computer Science* (2020 curriculum document).
>
> This file is a learning-oriented Markdown extraction of the official curriculum. It intentionally omits lecturer contacts, assessment matrices, repetitive grading percentages, long bibliography lists, page furniture, and administrative formatting. It is **not** a substitute for current IUH academic regulations.

## Program structure

The curriculum is organized across four semesters. Most taught courses carry **3 credits**; the Master Thesis carries **15 credits**.

## Course summaries

### Philosophy — `6012401`

**Purpose**

- Build a basic understanding of Western philosophy, Eastern philosophy and Marxist philosophy.
- Analyze relationships between philosophy and the sciences.
- Understand the role of science and technology in social development.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain major philosophical foundations covered by the course.
- Analyze philosophy-science relationships and their social role.
- Apply philosophical reasoning to science/technology development questions.

---

### English — `6011401`

**Purpose**

- Review B1-level grammar, sentence structures and vocabulary.
- Practice listening, speaking, reading and writing.
- Prepare learners for B1-format assessment and graduate-level communication needs.

**Key outcomes**

- Present personal information, participate in discussions and handle common speaking situations.
- Write emails, messages and structured letters.
- Read and understand B1-equivalent material using contextual and vocabulary strategies.

---

### Advanced Database — `6001111`

**Purpose**

Study advanced relational database topics and how database systems behave under storage, query-processing and concurrent workloads.

**Core content**

- File storage and organization.
- Indexing and hashing.
- Query optimization.
- Transaction management.
- Concurrency control using serializability, locking and timestamps.
- System/database recovery after transaction, system or storage failures.
- Distributed database concepts, distributed storage, advantages and limitations.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain advanced storage, indexing and hashing concepts.
- Analyze query optimization problems.
- Manage transactions and reason about concurrency control.

---

### Computational Statistics — `6001114`

**Purpose**

Provide statistical foundations for data analysis and data science.

**Core content**

- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Probability and common distributions: Binomial, Poisson, Normal, t, F and Chi-square.
- Confidence intervals.
- Hypothesis testing.
- Analysis of variance (ANOVA).
- Linear regression.

**Key outcomes**

- Apply EDA to a dataset.
- Understand common probability distributions.
- Calculate and interpret confidence intervals.
- Select and apply appropriate hypothesis tests.

---

### Advanced Artificial Intelligence — `6001115`

**Prerequisite:** Artificial Intelligence.

**Purpose**

Develop deeper AI knowledge and use modern tools/libraries to build intelligent systems.

**Core content / outcomes**

- Probability theory and graph-based representation of Bayesian networks.
- Learning methods for AI problems.
- Inference systems for sequential data.
- Recommender-system concepts.
- Application of AI knowledge to practical intelligent-system development.

---

### Advanced Algorithms — `6001127`

**Purpose**

Develop advanced algorithm-design and analysis skills, especially for difficult optimization problems.

**Core content**

- Algorithm design and analysis.
- Graph algorithms.
- Greedy methods / heuristics.
- Dynamic programming.
- NP-completeness.
- Approximation algorithms and approximation schemes.
- Metaheuristics, matheuristics and evolutionary approaches.

**Key outcomes**

- Analyze time complexity and algorithm performance.
- Evaluate algorithms for practical problems.
- Select appropriate algorithmic tools for a problem class.

---

### Advanced Data Mining — `6001130`

**Prerequisites:** Advanced Artificial Intelligence; Advanced Database.

**Purpose**

Study the knowledge-discovery process and advanced techniques for extracting useful patterns from data.

**Core content**

- Knowledge discovery / data-mining process.
- Data preparation for mining tasks.
- Classification.
- Association-rule mining.
- Clustering.
- Sequential-pattern mining.
- Maximal and closed frequent-set mining.
- Subgraph mining.
- Fuzzy-set and rough-set approaches.
- Use of software tools for practical mining problems.

**Key outcomes**

- Select appropriate mining techniques for a dataset/problem.
- Read and understand research literature in data mining.
- Independently investigate and solve a focused data-mining problem.

---

### Natural Language Processing — `6001122`

**Prerequisites:** Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning.

**Purpose**

Understand NLP fundamentals and apply NLP/deep-learning methods to real-world language problems.

**Core content**

- NLP concepts and terminology.
- Syntactic analysis.
- Semantic analysis.
- Language models from basic to more complex forms.
- NLP applications and real-world problem classification.

**Key outcomes**

- Analyze syntactic components and relationships in sentences.
- Compare and evaluate language models for a target language/problem.
- Analyze real NLP problems and choose suitable application approaches.

---

### Information Security & Safe — `6001121`

**Purpose**

Build foundational knowledge of information-system security and practical protection techniques.

**Core content**

- Security needs and common information-security problems.
- Encryption.
- Electronic/digital signatures.
- Hash functions.
- Authentication codes.
- Security protocols and secure-system operations.
- Practical security problem solving through individual/group work.

**Key outcomes**

- Present and analyze information-security problems.
- Apply security techniques to practical problems.
- Evaluate safety and complexity trade-offs of proposed solutions.

---

### Parallel Computing — `6001124`

**Purpose**

Develop understanding of parallel architectures, programming techniques, parallel algorithms and scientific applications.

**Core content**

- Parallel-system architectures.
- Parallel programming models.
- Parallel algorithms for numerical computation, search, optimization and machine-learning problems.
- Performance measurement of parallel computers and algorithms.
- Development of scientific applications across different parallel architectures.

**Key outcomes**

- Evaluate use cases for parallel computers.
- Analyze parallel performance terminology and metrics.
- Develop programs for different classes of parallel machines.

---

### Text and Web Analytics — `6001129`

**Purpose**

Apply data-mining concepts to text and web data.

**Core content**

- Text classification.
- Text-mining problems beyond classification.
- Web-data mining concepts.
- Web classification.
- Web-log analysis.
- Web-structure analysis.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain text/web mining problem types.
- Use analytical tools on text or web data.
- Solve and analyze practical text-mining and web-mining problems.

---

### Leadership Development — `6001210`

**Purpose**

Develop personal leadership capacity and practical skills for leading people and teams.

**Core content**

- Personal leadership philosophy.
- High-performance leadership and management.
- Employee evaluation, feedback and advising.
- Relationship building and influence.
- Conflict management.
- Working with different personalities.
- Goal management and creative problem solving.

**Key outcomes**

- Organize effective working relationships.
- Apply techniques for influencing others.

---

### Risk Analysis — `6001219`

**Purpose**

Understand organizational risk, expected risk levels, controls and management structures.

**Core content**

- Identification of risk components.
- Quantitative reasoning about risk.
- Risk-management structures and controls.
- Balancing risk and reward.
- Enterprise-wide decision and action considerations.

**Key outcomes**

- Identify organizational risk components.
- Measure expected risk levels.
- Explain how organizations structure controls to limit risk.

---

### Special Topic — `6001132`

**Purpose**

Train independent research skills through reading and analysis of scientific articles while preparing for thesis work.

**Core content / outcomes**

- Read and analyze scientific papers.
- Evaluate advanced or innovative methods, techniques and tools.
- Identify recent directions in Computer Science.
- Propose recommendations or research directions based on evidence.
- Consider ethical, industrial, economic and social context.
- Plan research work and thesis-related activities.

---

### Digital Image Processing — `6001131`

**Prerequisites:** Calculus 2, Programming Techniques, Data Structures and Algorithms.

**Purpose**

Build the mathematical and algorithmic foundation for digital image processing.

**Core content**

- Image representation and data structures.
- Image preprocessing and enhancement.
- Morphological processing.
- Segmentation.
- Image features and object representation.
- Design and implementation of image-processing algorithms.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain fundamental image-processing concepts and mathematical tools.
- Apply enhancement, morphology, segmentation and feature techniques.
- Design and implement practical image-processing algorithms.

---

### Deep Learning — `6001223`

**Prerequisite:** Artificial Intelligence.  
**Co-requisite in the source:** Machine Learning.

**Purpose**

Understand deep-learning approaches, applications and limitations and apply them to real problems.

**Core content**

- Machine-learning and deep-learning foundations.
- Neural networks.
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs).
- Reinforcement learning.
- Applications to Computer Vision, NLP and time-series forecasting.

**Key outcomes**

- Analyze deep-learning problem-solving principles.
- Analyze major neural-network families and learning approaches.
- Apply and justify deep-learning models for vision, language and time-series problems.

---

### Pattern Recognition and Analysis — `6001212`

**Prerequisites:** Statistics Computing and Applications; Advanced Artificial Intelligence.

**Purpose**

Study the design of pattern-recognition systems and major statistical/syntactic approaches.

**Core content**

- Pattern-recognition system design.
- Bayesian decision theory.
- Estimation theory.
- Linear discriminant analysis.
- Nonparametric techniques.
- Curse of dimensionality and theoretical design issues.
- Machine-learning applications in pattern recognition.
- Practical implementation using scientific Python tooling.

**Key outcomes**

- Analyze mathematical and algorithmic principles of pattern recognition.
- Design and implement practical recognition applications.
- Reason about research-oriented pattern-recognition problems.

---

### Scientific Research Methodology — `6013400`

**Purpose**

Provide foundations of scientific research and develop skills required for thesis work.

**Core content**

- Science and scientific-research concepts.
- Research methodology, process and methods.
- Academic reading and writing.
- Problem discovery and problem solving.
- Data collection and processing.
- Critical and logical thinking.
- Ethical, legal, commercial, industrial, economic and social context.
- Research planning for a Master's thesis.

**Key outcomes**

- Analyze and synthesize research-methodology knowledge for a concrete project.
- Read research papers effectively.
- Judge contextual and ethical constraints.
- Develop a research outline for a Master's thesis.

---

### Information Theory — `6001218`

**Purpose**

Study the fundamental limits of representing and transmitting information.

**Core content**

- Entropy.
- Mutual information.
- Communication models.
- Source-coding theorem.
- Channel-coding theorem.
- Applications to statistics, machine learning, economics, compression, signal processing, control and pattern recognition.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain core information-theory concepts.
- Determine and reason about quantities of information.
- Analyze representation/transmission limitations.

---

### Internet Technology of Modern Things — `6001220`

**Prerequisite:** Artificial Intelligence.  
**Co-requisite in the source:** Deep Learning.

**Purpose**

Apply AI techniques in IoT and automation systems across machine learning, robotics, embedded systems and communications.

**Core content**

- IoT systems and practical deployment problems.
- AI/deep-learning deployment on IoT, smart robots, cloud and mobile systems.
- Smart thermostat, autonomous-vehicle, intelligent-robot and Industrial IoT examples.
- Automation software systems.
- Startup/project planning around a deployed IoT system.

**Key outcomes**

- Analyze AI deployment issues in IoT-like environments.
- Deploy deep-learning models on connected/edge/cloud systems.
- Build a startup/project plan around an IoT solution.

---

### Data Processing on Cloud Computing — `6001222`

**Purpose**

Understand cloud-computing foundations with emphasis on data storage, processing and cloud database services.

**Core content**

- Cloud-computing overview, models and services.
- Virtualization and resource sharing/provisioning.
- Cloud storage and file systems.
- Cloud database technologies.
- Exposure to major cloud providers.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain cloud models and services.
- Understand virtualization/abstraction for shared resources.
- Choose and use cloud database/storage services appropriately.

---

### Big Data Analytics — `6001221`

**Purpose**

Understand Big Data architecture and use data-processing tools to build and optimize basic large-scale data-analysis systems.

**Core content**

- Big Data system architecture.
- Apache Pig / Pig Latin.
- Apache Hive / HiveQL.
- Spark SQL.
- Performance optimization with these tools.
- Design and implementation of a simple Big Data processing/analysis component.

**Key outcomes**

- Analyze components of a Big Data architecture.
- Design a simple processing component using Pig, Hive or Spark SQL.
- Implement Big Data analysis using the covered tools.

---

### Data Analysis Applications — `6001126`

**Purpose**

Apply analytical techniques, models and data-driven reasoning to decision-making problems.

**Core content**

- Data-analysis concepts and practices.
- Exploring and managing data.
- Modeling and algorithmic analysis.
- Prediction and decision support.
- Quantitative methods for organizational/managerial decisions.
- Evaluating results and informing future strategies.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain why data analysis matters for decision making and where it can fail.
- Understand changing data environments.
- Apply quantitative techniques, algorithms and models to decision-support problems.

---

### Data Visualization — `6001225`

**Purpose**

Transform computational information into effective visual forms for human perception, cognition and communication.

**Core content**

- Information and data visualization.
- Human perception and cognition.
- Computer graphics and human-computer interaction foundations.
- Numerical and non-numerical data visualization.
- Interaction with visualizations.
- Visual thinking and analytical process.

**Key outcomes**

- Explain the visual-analytics process.
- Analyze visual-thinking approaches.
- Use visualization tools to analyze and communicate data.

---

### Master Thesis — `6001229`

**Credits:** 15.

**Purpose**

Complete a scientific research project that demonstrates meaningful theoretical, experimental or practical contribution.

**Core activities**

- Analyze a complex Computer Science problem and formulate a research direction.
- Review and critically synthesize prior work.
- Propose and implement a solution.
- Design and conduct experiments/evaluation.
- Interpret research results.
- Write the thesis and scientific-paper-style material.

**Key outcomes**

- Present advanced Computer Science issues at depth.
- Apply computing and mathematical knowledge to a research problem.
- Produce meaningful scientific/practical contributions.
- Design, reason and experiment systematically.
- Demonstrate independent research and creativity.
- Communicate scientific work in a foreign language.
- Practice professional ethics, responsibility and collaboration.
- Develop a lifelong-learning plan related to the research direction.

## Curriculum relationships useful for the learning agent

These relationships come directly from prerequisite/co-requisite information and the curriculum layout:

- **Advanced AI → Advanced Data Mining**.
- **Advanced Database → Advanced Data Mining**.
- **AI / Machine Learning / Deep Learning → NLP**.
- **Statistics + Advanced AI → Pattern Recognition**.
- **AI + Deep Learning → IoT/modern connected-system applications**.
- **Scientific Research Methodology + Special Topic → Master Thesis preparation**.
