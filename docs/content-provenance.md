# Content sources and attribution

The technical topic outlines in [topic reading notes](../references/topic-notes.md)
were adapted from earlier condensed notes of the Faculty of Information Technology,
Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City: *Outline of Master Program in Computer
Science* (2020) and *PhD Program in Computer Science* (October 2022).

This describes the source lineage. It does not establish endorsement, permission to
redistribute the original documents or current degree requirements. The original PDFs
have not been independently reverified. Historical extracts remain in the public Git
history; this change does not remove that history.

The [topic map](../references/topic-map.md), practical lessons and labs are this
project's study material. Technical claims cite their relevant primary sources on the
lesson page. The lessons support rigorous self-study and research preparation;
completing them does not confer a master's or doctoral degree.

attribution and license terms.
