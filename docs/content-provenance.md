# Content sources and attribution

The self-study route follows the course-name inventory of Industrial University
of Ho Chi Minh City (IUH), *Outline of Master Program in Computer Science* (2020)
and *PhD Program in Computer Science* (October 2022). The inventory was checked
against the repository's historical condensed tables at commit
`fe7458371a480a7ac24b0537dded59367087a7ec`; the original PDFs have not been independently
reverified, and no current admission, credit or graduation rules are asserted.

[Course inventory](../references/topic-notes.md) retains factual names and codes,
including elective names. Its questions are newly written project prompts. The former
adapted syllabus descriptions have been removed from the active English and Vietnamese
notes. [Topic objectives and prerequisites](../references/topic-map.md) are project
design, rather than official IUH prerequisite rules.

Coverage measures published lesson artifacts for planned self-study objectives. It
records neither attendance nor proficiency. Doctoral preparation includes paper
criticism, reproduction, original-question design and writing; publications and a
thesis require actual research beyond delivery of daily lessons. This repository
awards no degree and claims no endorsement by IUH.

Original prose uses CC BY 4.0 and original code uses MIT; see [licensing](licensing.md).
Source documents and historical extracts are not relicensed. Git history has not been
rewritten. Technical lessons retain their own primary-source citations.
